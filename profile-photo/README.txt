PROFILE PHOTO
-------------
Put your photograph in this folder (JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF).
The most recently added photo becomes your profile photo on the Home page.
Older ones are ignored, so you can leave them here or delete them.

After adding a photo, double-click  update-photos.command  (in the main folder)
and refresh the website.

Tip: a portrait-style photo around 1200 x 1500 px works well.
