GALLERY
-------
Put photographs here and they appear in the Gallery on the Home page.

  - Photos placed directly in this folder are shown under "Moments".
  - To group photos by event, make a sub-folder and put them inside it.
    The folder name becomes the heading, e.g.
        gallery/Prometeo 2026/stage.jpg   ->  heading "Prometeo 2026"
        gallery/film-sets/day1.jpg        ->  heading "Film sets"
  - CAPTIONS: to put text in the footer of a photo, save a plain text file with
    the same name right next to it:
        gallery/stage.jpg   +   gallery/stage.txt   ->  the text in stage.txt
    appears at the bottom of that photo.
    Save the .txt as plain text (UTF-8); Hindi and other scripts work.
  - Newest photos come first. Every photo is shown at its true proportions.
    Clicking a photo does nothing; they are for viewing.

After adding or removing photos, double-click  update-photos.command
(in the main folder) and refresh the website.

Supported: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF. (Export HEIC photos as JPG.)
Tip: resize very large photos to about 2000 px on the long side so the page loads quickly.
