映画的構図と光の詩学

Visual narrative, cinematic framing, and educational media as mediums for articulating human emotion and natural physical aesthetics.

I. CINEMA

Filmmaking & Story

Scriptwriting and developing short narrative films that weave emotional arcs with philosophical inquiry. Studying character quietness, deliberate editing pace, and subtext.

II. VISUALS

Photography

Natural ambient lighting, intentional framing, and compositional perspective inspired by Japanese 2D animation directors (A Silent Voice, Makoto Shinkai). Capturing quiet, fleeting moments.

III. PEDAGOGY

Educational Media

Integrating concise narration, motion schematics, and clean typography to make scientific principles intuitive, avoiding sensory clutter through cognitive load reduction.

“Cinematography teaches visual economy—knowing what to leave out of the frame so what remains speaks with clarity. This is identical to thoughtful pedagogical design: removing extraneous cognitive noise so the underlying phenomenon is revealed.”
