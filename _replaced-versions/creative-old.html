Served as Assistant Head of the Media Team for Prometeo 2026, the flagship national technical festival of the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur. Managed visual identity execution across digital channels, physical festival stages, and student broadcasts.

  • Volunteer Mentorship & Leadership. Directed and coordinated an active cohort of 15+ student volunteers across pre-festival digital teaser campaigns and live multi-camera coverage during events.
  • Media Planning & Visual Strategy. Formulated structured production timelines, shot lists, camera crew dispatch routines, post-production editing pipelines, and high-turnaround highlight reels.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration. Partnered closely with publicity, stage logistics, guest relations, and design verticals to ensure unified narrative pacing and brand consistency across all public media.

Visual practice

Cinematography & Story

Visual narrative, cinematic framing, and educational media as mediums for articulating human emotion and natural physical aesthetics.

  1. I

    Filmmaking & StoryCinema

    Scriptwriting and developing short narrative films that weave emotional arcs with philosophical inquiry. Studying character quietness, deliberate editing pace, and subtext.

  2. II

    PhotographyVisuals

    Natural ambient lighting, intentional framing, and compositional perspective inspired by Japanese 2D animation directors (A Silent Voice, Makoto Shinkai). Capturing quiet, fleeting moments.

  3. III

    Educational MediaPedagogy

    Integrating concise narration, motion schematics, and clean typography to make scientific principles intuitive, avoiding sensory clutter through cognitive load reduction.

“Cinematography teaches visual economy—knowing what to leave out of the frame so what remains speaks with clarity. This is identical to thoughtful pedagogical design: removing extraneous cognitive noise so the underlying phenomenon is revealed.”— Aryan Mewa
