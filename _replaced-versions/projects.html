01 / NANOSCIENCE

Green Synthesis of Nanoparticles

環境調和型ナノ粒子合成

Plant-mediated synthesis protocol utilizing aqueous Neem leaf extract (Azadirachta indica) and optical characterization via UV–Visible spectroscopy.

Investigated the plant-mediated, eco-friendly green synthesis of metal-based nanoparticles utilizing aqueous extract from Neem leaves (Azadirachta indica) as an organic reducing and stabilizing agent. Conventional chemical nanoparticle synthesis relies on toxic reagents like sodium borohydride; this study explores sustainable, biologically benign alternative pathways.

Step-by-Step Experimental Methodology

実験手順概要
01
Plant Material Preparation: Fresh Neem leaves thoroughly washed with distilled water to remove particulate matter, shade-dried, and finely pulverized.
02
Aqueous Biomolecule Extraction: Powdered leaves stirred magnetically in deionized water at ~60°C for 4 hours to liberate active phytochemicals (terpenoids and polyphenols).
03
Micro-Filtration: Aqueous extract filtered through Whatman filter paper to obtain a clean, particulate-free liquid bio-reductant solution.
04
Precursor Reaction & Bio-Reduction: Aqueous extract combined with selected metal nitrate salts and heated to ~40°C for 1 hour to induce nucleation and reduction.
05
pH Titration & Colloidal Stabilization: pH carefully titrated to 8 using dilute sodium hydroxide (NaOH) to stabilize particle nucleation and prevent agglomeration.
06
Centrifugation, Drying & Calcination: High-speed centrifugation followed by oven drying and thermal calcination to isolate purified solid crystalline nanoparticles.
Precursor Salts Synthesized
  • • Iron(III) nitrate nonahydrate
  • • Cobalt(II) nitrate hexahydrate
  • • Zinc nitrate hexahydrate
  • • Magnesium nitrate
  • • Copper(II) nitrate trihydrate
  • • Nickel(II) nitrate hexahydrate
Spectroscopic Characterization

Optical absorption spectra and Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR) peaks were measured using UV–Visible spectroscopy to verify successful particle nucleation, determine optical bandgaps, and assess potential environmental and antimicrobial efficacy.

✓ 100% Non-toxic green synthesis pathway
02 / INTERACTIVE MEDIA

Scratch Interactive Quiz Game

対話型学習ゲーム設計

Gamified learning prototype exploring cognitive feedback loops, conditional logic blocks, and responsive user interaction.

Collaborators: Aman Raj, Herschelle Sharma, Aryan Mewa Computational Thinking Course Project

Developed as part of a Computational Thinking course project at IIT Jodhpur. The game translates core scientific principles into gamified, interactive logic blocks on Scratch, exploring how responsive interaction and immediate cognitive feedback reinforce retention for early STEM learners.

Instruction Sequencing

Configured conditional branches, question queuing, and scoring variables to guide student progression smoothly.

Adaptive Feedback

Immediate visual responses encourage persistence while scaffolding conceptual misconceptions without punitive friction.

Cognitive UI/UX

Clean, distraction-free visual layout designed to minimize extraneous cognitive load in compliance with CTML principles.

“Demonstrating how game-based computational structures can demystify formal scientific questions, converting dry problem sets into engaging empirical puzzles.”
