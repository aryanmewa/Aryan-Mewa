Personal Biography

学術への志向と表現

The intersection of analytical physical inquiry, laboratory investigation, and cinematic media.

I am an Integrated B.Sc.–B.Ed. student in Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur (IIT Jodhpur), driven by a deep interest in physical sciences, pedagogy, creative media, and educational technology. My work lies at the intersection of scientific inquiry, educational resource design, and visual storytelling.

My background spans classical and modern physics, mathematical methods, and contemporary educational theories. Beyond the laboratory and classroom, I explore filmmaking, photography, video editing, and interactive multimedia. I aim to connect theoretical physics with practical experiences, making scientific learning intuitive, inclusive, and engaging through hands-on experimentation, visual narrative, and technology.

“Science education is most impactful when complex, abstract ideas are translated into clear, engaging experiences that make discovery intuitive and accessible for all learners.”

My creative interests in photography and filmmaking inform my pedagogical perspective: good visual storytelling and good science teaching both rely on visual economy—clearing away extraneous distraction so the underlying physical phenomenon can be observed with clarity.

Learning Pursuits

継続的探求・研究計画

Expanding computational simulation and hands-on laboratory apparatus for low-resource schools.

01

Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning (CTML) in Physics

Applying Richard Mayer's multimedia design principles (modality, signaling, and temporal contiguity) to produce video modules for advanced wave mechanics and optical phenomena.

02

Low-Cost Tabletop Demonstration Apparatus

Prototyping low-cost physics demonstration kits using simple household materials and smartphone sensors, ensuring resource-limited schools can conduct empirical experiments.

03

Computational Simulation in Python

Extending numerical modeling in Python (NumPy, Matplotlib) to simulate time-dependent wavepacket diffusion, resonance in coupled oscillators, and electromagnetic field geometries.
